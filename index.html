ROME ITALY

Places to See

Flavian Amphitheatre

The Colosseum was originally name the Flavian Amphitheater. The name the Colosseum comes from a colossal bronze statue of Emperor Nero. It was built around 72 AD by Emperor Vespasian and finished by his son Titus in 80 AD.

Trevi Fountain

The Trevi Fountain name comes from tre vie which translates to three streets. It sits at the intersection of three roads. It was designed by Nicola Salvi in 1732 and complete by Giuseppe Pannini in 1762. It stands at 26.3 meters high and 49.15 meters wide. It is the largest Baroque fountain.

The Vatican City

The Vatican City is an independent city that sits within Rome, Italy. It is the home of the Pope and serves as the headquarters for the Roman Catholic Church. The structure was built from 1506 to 1626. Which it took St. Peter's Basilica 120 years to build.
